Questions from parents

Is Gappu Kids free?+

Yes. All games, stories, Kindness Missions and printables are free, with no ads and no sign-up.

What age is Gappu Kids for?+

Children aged 2 to 8. Stories and Kind or Not? are read aloud for little ones who can't read yet; Count with Gappu and ABC Pop get harder as children improve.

Is it safe for my child?+

There are no ads, no chat, no accounts and no personal data collected. Progress is saved only in your browser. A parent gate protects settings like the screen-time limit.

How do stars and badges work?+

Children earn stars for finishing games, stories and daily Kindness Missions. Stars unlock new caps for Gappu, and special achievements earn badges. Levels go up as stars are earned.

Does it work on phones?+

Yes — everything is made for touch screens first and works in any modern phone, tablet or computer browser.
