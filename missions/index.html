Kindness Missions

One small good deed every day — done in real life, not on a screen. When it’s done, a grown-up taps the button and Gappu gives ⭐ stars. Keep your 🔥 streak going!

30 kindness ideas for kids

A new mission appears every day. Want to try one right now? Pick any of these — every one is small enough for a toddler and meaningful at any age.

  1. 🍛Say “thank you” to the person who cooks your food.Let them say it at the table, looking at the cook.
  2. 🧸Share a toy or a snack with someone today.Praise the sharing, not the toy: “That was so kind!”
  3. 🍽️Help set the table for a meal.Spoons and napkins are perfect first jobs.
  4. 🤗Give someone in your family a big hug.Ask them who needs a hug the most today.
  5. 🌞Say “good morning” to three people.Count them together on your fingers.
  6. 🧺Put your toys back in the box — all by yourself.Make it a race against a two-minute song.
  7. 🖍️Draw a picture for someone you love.Help them write the person's name on it.
  8. 🪴Water a plant (with a grown-up).Talk about how plants need care, just like people.
  9. 🙏Say “please” every time you ask for something today.A gentle “What's the magic word?” works wonders.
  10. 🛍️Help carry the shopping bags.Give them the lightest bag and lots of thanks.
  11. 💬Tell someone one thing you like about them.Model it first: “I like how you laugh.”
  12. 🚪Let someone else go first today.Doors, lifts and queues are all good chances.
  13. 🐦Put out a bowl of water for birds or animals.Check it together tomorrow — did anyone drink?
  14. 🩹If you hurt someone, say sorry and help make it better.Sorry + a helping action is the whole lesson.
  15. 👕Help fold the clean clothes.Socks and small towels are the easiest start.
  16. 📞Call Dadi, Nani or a grandparent and say hello.Let them tell Grandma one thing they did today.
  17. 🗑️Put one piece of rubbish in the dustbin.Outside counts double — keep our streets clean.
  18. 😄Make someone laugh today.A funny face or a silly dance both count!
  19. 👋Say “thank you” to a helper — the driver, watchman or didi.Helpers often go unthanked. Let your child notice them.
  20. 👂Listen quietly when someone else is talking.Practise at dinner: one person talks, everyone listens.
  21. 👟Keep your shoes neatly in their place.A little habit that makes the whole home tidier.
  22. ⚽Ask someone, “Do you want to play with me?”Especially someone who is playing alone.
  23. 🧽Help clean up after dinner.Carrying their own plate to the sink is a great start.
  24. ⏳Wait patiently for your turn today.Name it when you see it: “You waited so well!”
  25. 🌟Give a friend a compliment.“You're a good friend because…” is a nice starter.
  26. 💡Switch off the lights when you leave a room.Being kind to our planet counts too.
  27. 📚Read a book together with a grown-up.Let your child turn the pages and pick the voice.
  28. 🙋Say “excuse me” instead of interrupting.Agree on a signal: a hand on your arm means “I'm waiting”.
  29. 🎁Pick a toy you don't play with to give to another child.Let them choose — giving should feel good, not forced.
  30. ❤️Say “I love you” to someone in your family.Say it back and watch them glow.
