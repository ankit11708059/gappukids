⭐ Good manners star chart

Catch them being kind! Each time your child uses a magic word, shares or waits their turn, they colour a star on today's row. Five rows, seven days, one page — stick it on the fridge where everyone can see it.

Free for home and classroom use. Fits one A4 page; in the print window choose “Save as PDF” to keep a copy.

Ideas for grown-ups

  • Start small: for little ones, colour stars together at bedtime while you talk about the day.
  • Praise the behaviour, not the star: “You shared your blocks with your brother. That was kind!”
  • A full row could earn a big hug, a story of their choice or a trip to the park — no sweets needed.
