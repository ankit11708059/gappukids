🦜 Parrot colouring page

Mithu is a quick, chatty parrot who always says thank you. Most parrots are bright green with a red beak, but this one can be any colour you like — rainbow parrots welcome!

⬇️ Download picture

Free for home and classroom use. Fits one A4 page; in the print window choose “Save as PDF” to keep a copy.

Ideas for grown-ups

  • Talk about real parrots: what colours are they? What sounds do they make?
  • Ask who your child would like to say thank you to today, and colour Mithu for them.
  • Cut it out and stick it on the fridge as a thank-you reminder.
