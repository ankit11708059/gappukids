🎨 A story about saying sorry

Gappu's Big Sorry

☀️
🌞

The full story (for grown-ups)

Mithu had drawn a beautiful picture of the sun in the sand. It had big, bright rays all around.

Gappu came running — thud, thud, THUD! — chasing a pretty butterfly.

SQUISH! His big foot landed right on Mithu's picture. The sun was all smudged.

Mithu's eyes filled with tears. Gappu wanted to run away and hide. His ears felt hot.

But Gappu stopped. He took a deep breath. “Oops! Gappu-sorry, Mithu. I wasn't looking. Can I help you draw a new one?”

Together they drew TWO suns — a big one and a small one. “Sorry fixed it!” smiled Mithu.

Moral of the story: When we make a mistake, saying sorry — and helping fix it — makes things right.

Talk about it together

  • What did Mithu draw in the sand?
  • What was Gappu chasing?
  • What did Gappu say to Mithu?
  • Can you remember a time you showed saying sorry like Gappu’s friends?
