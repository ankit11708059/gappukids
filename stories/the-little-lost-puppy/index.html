The Little Lost Puppy

☀️
👂🛒

On the way home from the market, Gappu heard a tiny sound. “Kuu… kuu…”

The full story (for grown-ups)

On the way home from the market, Gappu heard a tiny sound. “Kuu… kuu…”

Under a cart sat a little puppy, all alone and shivering. Its tummy was empty.

Some big animals walked past. “It's just a stray,” they said. But Gappu stopped.

Gappu gave the puppy a bowl of water and a piece of his roti. The puppy's tail went wag, wag, wag!

Gappu asked Amma for help. Together they made the puppy a warm, soft bed of straw.

Now the puppy — Gappu named him Motu — follows Gappu everywhere. Kindness made a brand-new best friend!

Moral of the story: Animals have feelings too. Being kind to them makes the world a better place.

Talk about it together

  • Where was the puppy?
  • What did Gappu give the puppy?
  • What did Gappu name the puppy?
  • Can you remember a time you showed kindness to animals like Gappu’s friends?
