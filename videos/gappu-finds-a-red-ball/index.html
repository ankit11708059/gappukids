Gappu Finds a Red Ball

Gappu found a shiny red ball. Whose is it? This 39-second cartoon story for kids aged 2–6 stars Gappu the baby elephant and Chiku the squirrel. It's a gentle lesson about honesty, told slowly in a calm voice so even the littlest viewers can follow. Read the story together below, then try the questions and the activity.

Read along

Gappu found a shiny red ball. Whose is it?

Gappu found a red ball under the big tree.

He wanted to keep it. Then he heard crying.

It was Chiku's ball! Gappu gave it back, gently.

Now they play with it together. Hooray!

💛 The lesson: Give back what is not yours.

Talk about it

Questions to ask after watching:

  • Whose ball did Gappu find? How did Chiku feel when it came back?
  • What should we do when we find something that isn't ours?

Try this today

Play “Whose is it?”: pick up three things around the house and give each one back to its owner.
