Gappu Won't Take a Bath!

Gappu does NOT want to take a bath! This 29-second cartoon story for kids aged 2–6 stars Gappu the baby elephant and Amma. It's a gentle lesson about keeping clean, told slowly in a calm voice so even the littlest viewers can follow. Read the story together below, then try the questions and the activity.

Read along

Gappu does NOT want to take a bath!

Splish, splosh! Gappu played in the mud all day.

Amma smiled. “Bath time is splash time!”

Whoosh! Gappu sprayed water with his trunk. So clean!

💛 The lesson: A bath keeps us clean and happy!

Talk about it

Questions to ask after watching:

  • Why did Gappu need a bath?
  • What is your favourite part of bath time?

Try this today

Draw a little routine chart together (brush, wash hands, bath) and tick each one today.
