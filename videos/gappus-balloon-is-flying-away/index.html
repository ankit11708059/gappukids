Gappu's Balloon Is Flying Away!

Oh no! Gappu's balloon is flying away! This 35-second cartoon story for kids aged 2–6 stars Gappu the baby elephant and Mithu the parrot. It's a gentle lesson about kindness, told slowly in a calm voice so even the littlest viewers can follow. Read the story together below, then try the questions and the activity.

Read along

Oh no! Gappu's balloon is flying away!

“Come back, balloon!” Up, up, up it went.

“I can fly! I'll catch it!” Let's cheer for Mithu! Say… FLY!

Flap, flap… GOT IT! Mithu brought it back! “Toot toot, hooray!”

💛 The lesson: Kind friends… always help.

Talk about it

Questions to ask after watching:

  • How did Gappu feel when his balloon flew away?
  • How did Mithu help? How can you help a friend who is sad?

Try this today

Do one kind thing for someone without being asked, then talk about how it felt.
