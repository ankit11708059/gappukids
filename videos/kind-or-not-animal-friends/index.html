Kind or Not? Animal Friends

Kind or not kind? Let's play! In this 47-second game for kids aged 2–6, Gappu shows little everyday moments and children shout “Kind!” or “Not kind!” — an easy way to start talking about kindness to animals.

Kind or not kind?

  • 🐦Kabir gives water to the thirsty birds.

    💛 Kind! Animals get thirsty too!

  • 🐕Riya throws a stone at a sleepy dog.

    ✋ Not kind. That hurts. Be gentle with animals.

Talk about it

Questions to ask after watching:

  • Was Kabir kind or not kind? What about Riya?
  • How can we be kind to the birds and dogs near our home?

Try this today

Put out a bowl of water for the birds or street animals, then watch quietly from a distance.
